# Author action required before submission

Do not submit the placeholders in the generated package.

1. Supply the definitive author list and order.
2. Supply every affiliation with full postal address and country.
3. Designate one corresponding author and supply full postal address and email.
4. Confirm acknowledgements or state that there are none.
5. Confirm the funding statement. If there was no specific funding, use Elsevier's recommended
   no-funding sentence.
6. Complete Elsevier's declarations tool, upload its DOCX, and insert the confirmed competing-
   interests statement.
7. Supply the CRediT contribution statement for every author.
8. Supply a biography of no more than 100 words for each author.
9. Confirm that all authors approve the manuscript, author order, AI-use declaration,
   originality statement, and submission to TFSC.
10. Add the submission date and corresponding-author signature to the cover letter.
11. Select the Technology Governance and Public Policy bureau unless the submitting author
    determines that another TFSC bureau is more appropriate.
12. Replace the internal author-action markers, rerun `make all`, and inspect the regenerated
    final package before upload.
