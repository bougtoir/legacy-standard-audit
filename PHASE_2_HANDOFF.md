## Novelty judgment

The defensible novelty is a pre-specified operational audit that reconstructs a
standard's historical decision problem and distinguishes current frictionless
regret from transition-adjusted regret under changed technological constraints.
It is a methodological synthesis and empirical protocol, not the first theory
of lock-in, standards, or socio-technical transition.

## Constraints carried forward

- Do not select cases by expected effect.
- Do not infer inefficiency from age or persistence.
- Do not aggregate incomparable outcomes into a universal index.
- Preserve null cases and insufficient-evidence classifications.
- Historical rationales require authoritative evidence.
- Practical redesign claims require switching, compatibility, safety,
  regulation, and uncertainty analysis.
