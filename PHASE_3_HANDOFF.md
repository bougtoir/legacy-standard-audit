# Phase 3 handoff: candidate universe

## Completed

- Generated 122 unique candidates across 12 domains.
- Covered built environment, transport, automotive interfaces, work,
  education, healthcare, packaging and consumer goods, paper and digital
  legacy, utilities and infrastructure, household and demographic systems,
  information-scarcity systems, and explicit null controls.
- Recorded each candidate's incumbent, history lead, technological and
  non-technological changes, decision variable, outcomes, data lead, switching
  and network costs, governance constraints, design-quality scores, and TFSC
  relevance.
- Kept all initial records at longlist status until case-specific sources were
  persisted and reviewed.

## Screening safeguard

Provisional and finalist scores concern evidence and research design only.
No confirmatory effect direction, magnitude, or statistical significance was
available to or used in selection.
