# Historical Claim Audit

## Decision

The frozen analyses do not identify a historical optimum for any case and never justify the equality \(x_{\mathrm{inc}}=x_h^*\). The formal historical optimum remains a conceptual benchmark. Empirically, the protocol begins from a historically defensible incumbent problem only to the extent supported by registered sources.

The machine-readable disposition is `research_inputs/historical_claim_audit.csv`.

## Source ceiling by case

### BE03

The DOE source supports formal office schedule assumptions, and the UCI source supports a current occupancy comparator. Neither reconstructs why a particular schedule arose, its original information set, or historical optimality. The manuscript therefore describes a specified nominal schedule, not an identified historical solution.

### WK07

NASA sources distinguish time-based, condition-based, and prognostics-based maintenance and document the simulated C-MAPSS setting. They do not establish that fixed-age replacement was historically optimal for an observed fleet. The permissible description is a historically defensible time-based incumbent problem with an unidentified historical optimum.

### UI01

The DOE report documents AMI-enabled time-based pricing and experimental customer-response studies. It does not identify why the analyzed fixed-price incumbent arose. The analysis remains bounded fixed-price partial-equilibrium arithmetic without a historical-origin claim.

### UI09

FAO-56 and the sensor-review metadata support contemporary reference-crop and sensor-based scheduling methods. They do not reconstruct the origin or optimality of a calendar irrigation rule. The result is limited to a calendar comparator and a perfect-weather reference-crop benchmark.

### TR01

The FHWA manual defines pre-timed and actuated control. The NYC data do not identify a verified intersection, installed controller, chronology, or deployed timing plan. The case remains a directional delay proxy with insufficient evidence.

### NC01 and NC03

The ISO pages verify current standard metadata, not origin narratives, installed-base quantities, or historical optimality. NC01 is an evidence-gap compatibility control. NC03 is a null only for repeated-halving similarity.

## Global corrections

- Replaced language about conditions “under which an incumbent was formed” with conditions relevant to an incumbent decision.
- Made the information-scarcity expectation conditional on source support.
- Added an explicit statement that \(x_h^*\) is conceptual and \(x_{\mathrm{inc}}=x_h^*\) is not assumed.
- Replaced “reconstruct the historical objective” with “state a historically defensible incumbent problem and the limits of reconstruction.”
- Revised Figure 1 and the cover letter to use the same evidence ceiling.
- Retained general lock-in, installed-base, path-dependence, and coordination statements only as cited theory, not as case findings.

## Residual limitation

The candidate registry contains screening-era hypotheses about origins, objectives, and constraints. Those fields are preserved because they are frozen design records, but they are not verified confirmatory findings and must not be promoted into manuscript claims without new registered historical evidence.
