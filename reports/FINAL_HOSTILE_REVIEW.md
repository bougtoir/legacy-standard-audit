# Final hostile review

## 1. TFSC handling editor

**Material criticism:** The submission could still look like a collection of incomparable
examples, and its procedural contribution could be mistaken for claiming invention of
standards review.

**Disposition:** Fixed. The introduction now distinguishes the protocol from ISO systematic
review and NIST FACTS. Figure 3 synthesizes audit states rather than effect magnitudes. The
abstract, discussion, and conclusion state that heterogeneity tests portability and prohibits
population or pooled-effect inference.

## 2. Technology-transition scholar

**Material criticism:** Capability change is not itself a transition theory, and the manuscript
could understate institutional co-evolution.

**Disposition:** Fixed in framing. The paper explicitly disclaims a new general transition or
lock-in theory. Theory and discussion treat institutions, infrastructure, user practices,
safety, regulation, complementors, and installed bases as transition constraints. The output is
a re-audit classification, not a transition prediction.

## 3. Standards and path-dependence scholar

**Material criticism:** Periodic review, lifecycle analysis, compatibility, and lock-in are
established ideas.

**Disposition:** Fixed. The introduction identifies those antecedents and limits novelty to
their integration in a frozen empirical evidence sequence with null retention and a redesign
gate. Historical optimality is not inferred from persistence.

## 4. Optimization reviewer

**Material criticism:** “Regret” could be read as a common or pooled optimization quantity,
while thresholds could be mistaken for welfare criteria.

**Disposition:** Fixed. Every objective, feasible set, denominator, comparator, and unit remains
case-specific. Thresholds are declared operational audit labels, not significance tests or
universal welfare rules. No pooled magnitude is estimated. The historical optimum is
conceptual and unidentified in the cases.

## 5. Empirical skeptic

**Material criticism:** The two material labels depend on perfect-information comparators, and
the case set may manufacture a positive result.

**Disposition:** Fixed. Oracle/proxy status is adjacent to every result. Joint removal of WK07
and UI09 leaves zero material primary labels; excluding simulated C-MAPSS leaves one. Null,
small, and insufficient cases remain frozen. No realized deployment effect or prevalence claim
is made.

## 6. Reproducibility and integrity reviewer

**Material criticism:** Cross-artifact title drift, manual numerical transcription, mutable
sources, or uncited outputs could invalidate the package.

**Disposition:** Fixed. The supplement imports the canonical title. Critical values,
classifications, intervals, design counts, transition levels, and strongest-case removal are
machine-checked against regenerated outputs. Raw snapshots, hashes, and DOI metadata are
persisted. Main and supplementary figures and tables are cited. A clean build and repeated
rebuild preserve analytical outputs.

## Residual limitations accepted rather than “fixed”

- No Level A transition evidence exists.
- No case identifies a realized causal deployment effect.
- Historical optima are not reconstructed.
- The purposive heterogeneous cases do not estimate prevalence.
- Office, PDF, and ZIP container hashes may vary with build metadata; analytical CSV/JSON and
  rendered PNG outputs are stable.

These limitations define the evidence ceiling and should not be concealed by additional
modeling.
