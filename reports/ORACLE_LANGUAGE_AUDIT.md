# Oracle and Proxy Language Audit

## Scope and method

Every occurrence of **oracle, adaptive, optimal/optimum, saving, regret, improvement, material, redesign,** and **benefit** was inventoried after regeneration in:

- the anonymized manuscript (no separate supplement is submitted);
- cover letter;
- highlights;
- manuscript and separate-table captions and cells;
- editable figures and graphical abstract.

The complete paragraph-, cell-, line-, and slide-level inventory is `outputs/audits/oracle_language_occurrences.csv`; counts by artifact and term are in `outputs/audits/oracle_language_summary.csv`. The inventory is generated by `scripts/audit_oracle_language.py`.

## Language rules applied

1. **Oracle/perfect information:** name the unavailable information directly and place simulation, proxy, or perfect-information status beside the result.
2. **Adaptive/responsive:** use only for the policy class or technological capability; do not imply a deployed controller.
3. **Optimal/optimum:** reserve for an explicitly solved objective and feasible set. Do not state or imply that the incumbent was historically optimal.
4. **Saving/benefit/improvement:** specify the arithmetic, proxy, unit, baseline, and omitted implementation or behavioral response. Do not use these terms for realized welfare or deployment outcomes.
5. **Regret:** retain where it is the defined within-case objective difference; distinguish static regret from a transition-adjusted estimate.
6. **Material:** retain only as a pre-specified decision label, not a universal welfare judgment or effect-size category.
7. **Redesign:** distinguish evidence sufficient to trigger re-audit from evidence sufficient to recommend implementation.

## Case-specific dispositions

### BE03

Permitted language:

- perfect-label occupancy comparator;
- nominal illuminated minute-observations;
- nominal on-time reduction;
- oracle technical upper bound where explicitly qualified.

Prohibited interpretation:

- measured electricity or cost saving;
- deployed sensor performance;
- identified false-positive/false-negative performance;
- transition-adjusted superiority.

### WK07

Permitted language:

- C-MAPSS simulated trajectories;
- perfect remaining-life oracle;
- static regret in normalized cost units;
- pre-specified material-mismatch threshold met under oracle comparison.

Prohibited interpretation:

- observed fleet savings;
- deployed predictive-maintenance effectiveness;
- safety, reliability, certification, or regulatory benefit;
- transition-adjusted superiority.

### UI01

Permitted language:

- bounded 1% intraday reallocation;
- fixed observed-price arithmetic saving;
- partial-equilibrium technical benchmark.

Prohibited interpretation:

- realized customer saving;
- tariff response;
- equilibrium wholesale-price effect;
- deployable AMI business case.

### UI09

Permitted language:

- perfect-weather reference-crop comparator;
- normalized objective regret under the stated fivefold shortfall penalty;
- pre-specified material-mismatch threshold met under oracle comparison.

Prohibited interpretation:

- physical water saving;
- deployable agronomic optimum;
- crop-yield, welfare, or monetary benefit;
- transition-adjusted superiority.

### TR01

Permitted language:

- directional demand-allocation delay proxy;
- 27.2% proxy reduction under the retained calculation;
- insufficient evidence.

Prohibited interpretation:

- actual traffic-signal redesign effect;
- intersection-level delay, queue, pedestrian, emissions, or safety benefit.

## Residual audited uses

All residual uses are either:

- formal within-case metrics, such as static regret;
- pre-specified raw classification labels that explicitly include “under oracle benchmark”;
- generic theory or capability terms, such as adaptive control;
- bibliographic titles, including cost-benefit and no-regret source titles;
- ordinary non-empirical usage, such as the coordination benefits of standards or supplementary material.

No residual occurrence states or implies a realized deployment effect, observed fleet saving, physical UI09 water saving, actual TR01 signal redesign effect, or transition-adjusted point estimate.

## Phase decision

Oracle and proxy language is globally disciplined for the current generated package. Later narrative revision may shorten or reorganize these statements, but it must preserve the evidence ceiling and case-specific prohibited interpretations above.
