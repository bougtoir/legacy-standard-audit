# Critical review before manuscript construction

Review date: 2026-09-24

This review applies the standards of a skeptical TFSC editor, methods reviewer,
and reproducibility reviewer before figures, prose, and submission files are
constructed.

## Highest priority: mandatory before submission

### The empirical cases do not reconstruct historical optima

- **Fatality:** desk-reject or major-revision risk.
- **Correction value:** very high.
- **Feasibility:** addressable by narrowing the claim; not addressable with the
  current datasets as a full historical reconstruction.
- **Required correction:** present the empirical analyses as frozen
  present-day audit demonstrations. Treat historical-objective reconstruction
  as a required protocol step that is only partially evidenced here, not as an
  empirical achievement for every case.

### Transition-adjusted regret is not estimated

- **Fatality:** major-revision risk because the framework makes switching and
  coordination costs central.
- **Correction value:** very high.
- **Feasibility:** new case-specific cost data would be required.
- **Required correction:** report static technical benchmarks and explicit
  break-even identities only. Withhold redesign recommendations and state that
  rational persistence cannot be rejected where transition costs are absent.

### Oracle comparators are not deployable interventions

- **Fatality:** major-revision risk if interpreted causally.
- **Correction value:** very high.
- **Feasibility:** addressable in labeling and claims.
- **Required correction:** label BE03, WK07, UI09, and TR01 as oracle or proxy
  upper bounds wherever reported. Never describe them as realized savings,
  observed policy effects, or safety benefits.

### The selected cases do not support a population effect

- **Fatality:** major-revision risk if generalized.
- **Correction value:** high.
- **Feasibility:** addressable in design and discussion.
- **Required correction:** retain the pre-specified cases as a designed audit
  set, not a random sample. Do not pool magnitudes or make causal claims about
  legacy standards as a population.

## High priority

### Temporal uncertainty should preserve serial dependence

- **Fatality:** material methods concern.
- **Correction value:** high.
- **Feasibility:** addressable with existing data.
- **Required correction:** use block rather than independent-observation
  bootstrap intervals for BE03, UI01, UI09, and TR01. Treat the WK07 engine as
  the independent sampling unit.

### UI01 does not observe a flat retail tariff or customer response

- **Fatality:** major interpretation concern.
- **Correction value:** high.
- **Feasibility:** addressable by precise labeling.
- **Required correction:** describe the comparison as the observed load profile
  versus bounded price-aware intraday shifting at fixed observed wholesale
  prices. Do not claim an estimated retail-tariff response.

### UI09 depends on normative loss weights and omitted agronomy

- **Fatality:** major interpretation concern.
- **Correction value:** high.
- **Feasibility:** partly addressable with sensitivity analysis.
- **Required correction:** foreground sensitivity to shortfall penalties and
  calendar estimators. Treat the result as a reference-crop/perfect-weather
  benchmark, not irrigation advice.

### TR01 lacks an identified incumbent signal plan

- **Fatality:** major interpretation concern.
- **Correction value:** high.
- **Feasibility:** not addressable without additional local data.
- **Required correction:** retain supplementary and insufficient-evidence
  status; do not use it to support redesign.

## Medium priority

### Classifications are decision labels, not statistical discoveries

- **Correction value:** moderate.
- **Required correction:** explain that classifications synthesize effect
  magnitude, uncertainty, source quality, and omitted transition constraints;
  they are not hypothesis-test outcomes.

### BE03 schedule sensitivity is substantively large

- **Correction value:** moderate.
- **Required correction:** show the full 06:00–20:00, 07:00–19:00, and
  08:00–18:00 sensitivity rather than privileging the central schedule.

### NC01 is an evidence-gap control, not proof of rational lock-in

- **Correction value:** moderate.
- **Required correction:** use NC01 to demonstrate that compatibility claims
  cannot be quantified from dimensions metadata alone.

### NC03 validates only one geometric objective

- **Correction value:** moderate.
- **Required correction:** state that the zero-loss result does not evaluate
  manufacturing, screen use, accessibility, or other paper-system objectives.

## Optional improvements

- Add observed implementation-cost evidence for one primary case and estimate a
  genuinely transition-adjusted result.
- Add a verified historical control document for each incumbent.
- Replicate the audit in a prospectively selected second case set.

## Gate decision

Manuscript construction may proceed only with the mandatory claim narrowing,
block-bootstrap correction, and case-specific limitations above. The current
evidence does not support a general claim that persistent standards are
obsolete, nor any practical redesign recommendation.
