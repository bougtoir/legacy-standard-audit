# Final editor audit

## Editorial fit

- **Technology centrality: pass.** The manuscript's mechanisms are sensing, computation,
  communication, prognostics, interval metering, and adaptive control. Social and
  institutional implications follow from those technological changes rather than replacing
  them.
- **Contribution: pass with narrow positioning.** The defensible novelty is a frozen,
  falsifiable measurement and governance protocol. It is not a claim to originate lock-in,
  path dependence, standards battles, switching costs, or transition governance.
- **Article type: pass.** The package is structured as a full research article with theory,
  methods, heterogeneous empirical demonstrations, falsification, discussion, implications,
  limitations, data availability, declarations, and references.
- **Methodological transparency: pass.** Case selection is frozen; public inputs are
  snapshot-preserved and checksum-registered; numerical manuscript values are generated;
  null and insufficient-evidence cases remain in the analysis.
- **Journal mechanics: pass subject to author metadata.** The abstract, keywords, highlights,
  anonymized manuscript, separate title page, editable tables, separate figures, supplement,
  data statement, AI disclosure, and graphical abstract are present.

## Desk-rejection risks

1. The title's phrase “obsolete optimization problems” can be read as an empirical conclusion,
   although the manuscript uses it to describe altered constraints rather than to label every
   incumbent obsolete.
2. The demonstrations do not fully reconstruct every historical optimum and do not estimate a
   full transition-adjusted case.
3. Four comparators are oracle or proxy bounds rather than deployable interventions.
4. The heterogeneous purposive case set cannot establish a population frequency or average
   magnitude of mismatch.

These risks are disclosed in the abstract, methods, discussion, limitations, conclusion, and
claim-evidence ledger. The manuscript consistently presents the analyses as demonstrations of
the audit rather than evidence that legacy standards should generally be replaced.

## Editorial disposition

Scientifically reviewable with high but explicit scope limitations. Do not submit until author
names, affiliations, corresponding-author information, funding, and competing-interest
statements are supplied and confirmed.
