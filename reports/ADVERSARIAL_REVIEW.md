# Adversarial reviewer audit

## Overall assessment

The paper offers a reproducible protocol and an unusually disciplined treatment of negative
evidence. It does not establish that legacy standards are generally inefficient, and its
strongest empirical estimates are oracle benchmarks. Its value therefore depends on keeping the
methodological claim narrower than the motivating rhetoric.

## Major challenges and dispositions

1. **Historical optima are incompletely reconstructed.**
   - Disposition: retained as a major limitation. The manuscript calls the cases current-audit
     demonstrations and does not claim complete historical identification.
2. **Transition-adjusted regret is not numerically estimated.**
   - Disposition: unresolved by available data and not imputed. Redesign recommendations are
     withheld; the limitation is central to the decision rule.
3. **Oracle comparators are not deployable interventions.**
   - Disposition: every affected case is labeled as an upper bound or proxy. The manuscript
     avoids causal deployment language.
4. **The purposive case set does not support population inference.**
   - Disposition: no pooled effect, prevalence estimate, or population causal claim is made.
     Leave-one-case/domain-out results are reported only as descriptive falsification.
5. **Classification thresholds are normative.**
   - Disposition: thresholds are now explicit, configured before regeneration, and described as
     operational decision labels rather than hypothesis tests or universal welfare criteria.
6. **Temporal intervals could be anticonservative under serial dependence.**
   - Disposition: temporal cases use circular block resampling with configured block sizes.
     Structural uncertainty remains unquantified and is disclosed.
7. **UI01 lacks a retail tariff and behavioral response model.**
   - Disposition: the estimate is restricted to partial-equilibrium procurement arithmetic with
     fixed prices.
8. **UI09 is driven by loss weights and omits agronomy.**
   - Disposition: loss-weight and calendar-rule sensitivity are reported; crop, soil, efficiency,
     and yield omissions are explicit. The result is not presented as irrigation advice.
9. **TR01 does not identify the incumbent control problem.**
   - Disposition: the case remains supplementary and classified as insufficient evidence.
10. **NC01 and NC03 could be overgeneralized.**
    - Disposition: NC01 is an evidence-gap compatibility control, not proof of rational lock-in;
      NC03 is restricted to repeated-halving geometry.

## Recommendation

The manuscript is defensible as a methods-and-governance article if the title and abstract are
read together with the explicit non-obsolescence conclusion. The appropriate reviewer verdict is
major revision rather than rejection if the journal expects a fully estimated
transition-adjusted empirical case; otherwise the protocol is reviewable in its current narrowed
form.
